MSc dissertation · 2026
Structured radiology reports from 3D chest CT

Question. Can a vision-language model write a structured chest CT report, and is it reading the scan?

Built. Frozen ResNet-34, VGG-16 and Med3D encoders, a Q-Former-style bridge and BioGPT-Large with LoRA, trained on an NVIDIA A100. Blank-input and shuffled-scan controls, three seeds and a paired bootstrap.

Every encoder scored 0.09 to 0.12 BLEU-4, and so did a model trained on blank images. The score came from the language model, not the scan, which places the bottleneck in the frozen encoder.

 AI as a Medical Device · module mark 85%
Glioma grade from tumour mutations

Question. Can a small gene panel separate low-grade glioma from glioblastoma without missing the aggressive cases?

Built. A leak-free scikit-learn pipeline on 861 TCGA patients, four model families, threshold analysis, and an MHRA and NHS deployment strategy.

Logistic regression on a 10-gene panel caught 64 of 73 glioblastomas in the held-out test set. Chosen on missed-cancer risk, not accuracy.

Breast cancer classification workflow

Question. How far can a disciplined workflow push sensitivity on a small diagnostic dataset?

Built. Imbalance handling, feature selection, Naive Bayes, random forest, SVM and a small PyTorch network, with cross-validation and GridSearchCV.

The tuned SVM reached 0.94 cross-validated accuracy, and a 0.3 decision threshold caught every malignant case in the held-out split at 0.84 specificity.

 Medical Imaging and AI · 2026
Brain MRI image processing case study

Question. Which classical processing steps improve a T1 brain MRI with a focal lesion, and which only appear to?

Built. Denoising, CLAHE, Otsu thresholding, Canny edges and morphology, each step measured with SNR, CNR, PSNR and SSIM. Plus five labs on CT, MRI, radiography and microscopy.

Bilateral filtering cut white-matter noise by 41%. Histogram equalisation inverted the lesion contrast, while CLAHE preserved structure (SSIM 0.77 against 0.48).
